# ChineseEcho Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 25, 2026**

ChineseEcho is a macOS Chinese-language learning application.

## Data stored on your Mac

ChineseEcho stores vocabulary, dictation sets, learner hints, practice
history, review schedules, generated example sentences, and preferences
locally on your Mac. This information is not uploaded to the developer.

User-created backups are saved only to a location selected by the user.

## Speech and notifications

Dictation speech is produced through macOS speech services. Review
notifications are scheduled locally through macOS. ChineseEcho does not
operate a server that receives vocabulary, speech content, or notification
history.

## Optional Local AI

If you choose to install Local AI, ChineseEcho downloads model files from
Hugging Face. The request may disclose ordinary network information, such
as your IP address, to Hugging Face. Hugging Face’s handling of that
information is governed by its own privacy policy.

After installation, ChineseEcho generates vocabulary details and example
sentences locally on your Mac. Learning content is not sent to the
developer for generation.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ChineseEcho does not contain advertising, cross-app tracking, or
developer-operated analytics.

## Retention and deletion

Learning data remains on your Mac until you delete it or remove the app’s
stored data. Downloaded Local AI files can be deleted using **Settings →
Local AI → Remove Model**. Individual vocabulary and dictation sets can be
deleted inside ChineseEcho.

## Children’s privacy

ChineseEcho does not knowingly collect personal information from children
or other users.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when ChineseEcho’s features or data practices
change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
